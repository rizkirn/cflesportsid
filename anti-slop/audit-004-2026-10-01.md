## Finding

1. **HIGH, R-27: no explicit pending indicator during form submission.**
   The server-rendered form navigates through the browser's normal loading state,
   but does not change the submit label or announce that creation is in progress.
   Evidence: `src/pages/admin/tournaments/new.astro` uses a native POST form with
   no pending-state script. Suggested fix: progressive enhancement that announces
   "Creating tournament" and prevents repeat clicks while the request is pending,
   retaining native form behavior when JavaScript is unavailable.
   Status: awaiting selection of finding #1; no audit fixes applied.

the CFL logo, condensed headings, orange actions, olive/charcoal surfaces, and
