capacity counter, atomic save, and read-only Bracket Setup checkpoint.

## Data, security, and limits

Real-schema tests cover duplicate/missing/colliding teams, capacity, rollback of
both new-team inserts and memberships, stable IDs, stale forms, concurrent setup
changes and creation collisions, locking after matches/BYEs/start, and preservation
of global teams. No BYEs, matches, players, or roster records are created.
185 source records validate with zero errors and warnings. Built D1 and fallback
servers reject GET and same-origin POST for list/create/setup/participants/bracket.
No remote staging or production database writes were performed in verification.

The public team catalog/profiles still use legacy Astro content. Staging team
creation does not publish profiles or mutate JSON; this read-layer migration is
outside Participants v1. Bracket Setup is a saved-participant checkpoint, not a
draw/seeding implementation. Physical mobile keyboards, browser zoom, and a
screen-reader session were not tested and are not claimed as evidence.
