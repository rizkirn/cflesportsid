## Change

An explicit backend POST copies current team players into the submitted workspace
draft when no saved roster or usable latest earlier tournament source exists.
An earlier source remains preferred and existing participant copy behavior stays
intact. Copy does not write players or tournament_rosters. Unsaved feedback and
editable Tournament IGN lead to Save Roster. Saved records cannot be replaced by
copy, including a forged request that omits their form entries. Drafts belonging
to other teams and edited IGN values are preserved. Conflicts, capacity, stale
revisions and preparation locks use existing validation. No historical claim or
S1/S2 backfill is introduced.
