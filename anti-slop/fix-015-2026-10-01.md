# Phase A: Live result controls

Design read: keep the established CFL esports dark palette and compact admin round rows. The work is a staff operation screen: scores are the focal point, an explicit save publishes them, and a separate confirmation advances the bracket. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Reuse the current type, orange action accent, spacing, and bracket identity; no new assets or decorative system.

Scope: added score controls and their public score/detail-empty states. Existing unrelated site design is retained. Test teams are explicitly named Offline test fixtures and exist only in the isolated local database.
