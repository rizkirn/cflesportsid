# Phase B: Match Details Editor

Design read: this staff editor replaces the sheet between three screenshots and
published match statistics. Keep the established CFL admin palette, type, orange
action accent and rectangular controls. Map results lead; roster tables follow.
ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. No new visual assets or framework are needed.
Astro renders the editor, with a small TypeScript interaction layer.
