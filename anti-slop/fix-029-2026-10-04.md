## Changes and purpose

Shared admin tokens use graphite surfaces, readable UI typography, neutral
dividers and modest radii. Orange marks primary actions and active navigation;
green/blue/yellow/red communicate ready/info/pending/destructive states. Tables
and expanded rosters are flat. Dialogs and dirty-state shelves retain elevation
because they mark a separate editing or confirmation boundary.

Overview is an operational landing page: independent compact tournament rows,
existing Participants/Roster/Maps/Bracket state, next-action links and flat
Needs Attention rows. Recent completed tournaments remain compact. Database
links are secondary. No KPI cards or invented backend state. Existing read
helpers supply the presentation; existing server validation remains authoritative.
At most eight active and two recent completed tournaments are shown, with a
visible count and All Tournaments link when the active list is truncated.

Applied to navigation, workspace identity, master forms/tables, Participants,
Roster, official Maps, admin-scoped native tools, bracket/result dialogs,
Matches, guided and read-first Match Details. Progression is natural concise
copy; empty Result History stays hidden. Guided map states have a full active
surface and semantic completed state. Backend, schema, public tool sources,
historical selection and persistence behavior are frozen.
